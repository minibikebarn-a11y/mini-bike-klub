Mini Bike Maintenance: A Beginner's Guide

A little routine maintenance keeps a custom mini bike running the way it looked on day one.

Before every ride

Check tire pressure, chain tension, and that all fasteners on the frame and body panels are tight.

Every few rides

Inspect the engine oil level, clean the air filter, and look over the brakes for wear.

Seasonal care

Before long-term storage, drain fuel or add a stabilizer, and keep the bike covered to protect the paint and components.
